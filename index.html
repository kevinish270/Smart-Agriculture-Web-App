About AgriSmart

AgriSmart is a web-based agriculture platform developed to help farmers, buyers, cooperatives, and agricultural organizations improve farming through digital technology. The platform provides weather forecasts, market prices, modern farming techniques, and direct communication between farmers and buyers.

The Problem

Many farmers still depend on traditional farming methods. They often lack accurate weather information, updated crop prices, and access to larger markets. As a result, productivity decreases while post-harvest losses increase.

AgriSmart solves these challenges by giving farmers one platform where they can receive weather updates, market information, modern farming knowledge and communicate directly with buyers.

Mission & Vision
