Smart Agriculture Web App

Empowering farmers through real-time data and direct market access.

The Problem

Farmers often struggle with unpredictable weather, poor access to market prices, and limited knowledge of modern farming techniques. This leads to low productivity, wasted resources, and reduced income. Many rely on word-of-mouth or outdated methods, which makes farming less efficient and less profitable.

A web application can solve this by providing farmers with real-time weather forecasts, up-to-date market prices, and access to agricultural best practices. The app could also include a platform for farmers to connect with buyers directly, reducing middlemen and increasing profits. By empowering farmers with information, the app would improve food security and strengthen the agricultural economy.

Internal Users

  • Farmers: Access weather, prices, and tips.
  • Cooperatives: Coordinate and share resources.
  • Local Buyers: Benefit from direct trade.

External Partners

  • Meteorological Agencies
  • Government Agricultural Boards
  • Market Regulators
  • NGOs & Int. Organizations
